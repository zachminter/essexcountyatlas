Essex County, New Jersey, decade by decade 🗺️

About

These maps follow how Essex County changed from 1940 to 2020, using census tract data from IPUMS NHGIS.

  • Essex County: the county and its municipalities.
  • Race by decade: percent white by census tract.
  • Education by decade: share of adults with four or more years of college.
  • Income by decade: median household income by tract compared with the US median.
  • Highways: the highway network through the county.

About the data

Before 1960, census tracts covered only Newark, so the 1940 and 1950 maps show the city alone. The census did not publish tract-level race counts for 1950. Questions and definitions also change from one census to the next, so compare decades with care.
